## 2025-02-28 - [Vectorized Cosine Similarity & Global Stop Words]
**Learning:** `get_tokens()` in `main.py` recalculates `stop_words = set(stopwords.words('english'))` and re-compiles regex repeatedly, dominating processing time for large resume arrays. In addition, computing cosine similarities in a loop over each resume vector is significantly slower than doing one vectorized call like `cosine_similarity(jd_vector, tfidf_matrix[1:])[0]`. Pre-calculating NLTK stop words globally and vectorizing cosine similarity provides over an order of magnitude speedup in similarity calculations.
**Action:** Extract constant computations out of repeatedly called functions. Replace loop-based Scipy/Scikit-Learn calls with vectorized operations (`tfidf_matrix[1:]`). Ensure matrix slices stay 2D when required by Scikit-Learn (`[0:1]` instead of `[0]`).
