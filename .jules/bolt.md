## 2024-03-21 - [Optimize tokenization and similarity calculation]
**Learning:** Initializing NLTK stopwords and regex compilation inside processing loops adds massive unnecessary overhead per document. Similarly, iterating over each resume manually to calculate cosine similarity is slower than letting `scikit-learn` use optimized matrix operations on all vectors simultaneously. Lastly, extracting `.text` attributes in lists sequentially rather than via list comprehensions slows down processing due to Python's object lookup time.
**Action:** Move regex compilation and `stopwords.words('english')` initialization to the global/module level. Instead of iterating `cosine_similarity(jd, resume)` over `N` resumes, slice the TF-IDF matrix `tfidf_matrix[0:1]` vs `tfidf_matrix[1:]` and perform a vectorized computation. Pre-calculate list data with list comprehensions before entering business logic loops.
